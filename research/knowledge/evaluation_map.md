**General pattern:** Single-node (often single-GPU) A10/A40/A6000 evaluations dominate eviction/compression papers; long-context (≥100K) claims often extrapolate from ≤8K real + synthetic scaling via simulator (Splitwise, DistServe, Llumnix) or dummy models (Mooncake).
