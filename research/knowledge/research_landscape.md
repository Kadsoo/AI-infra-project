# 1. Field Overview

- Field is LLM inference KV-cache optimization spanning system and algorithm layers, organized around 9-14 technical dimensions from memory allocation to distributed serving and workload-aware policy (taxonomy_draft.md; stage1_summary.md; papers.md).
- Corpus of 43 papers (38 deduped +5 auditor supplements, 2022-2026; 31 High /12 Medium; 63% 2024-2026 recent; 8 Foundational) shows shift from serving foundations (2022-2023) to eviction/compression (2023-2024) to RAG/agent/heterogeneous and disaggregated pools (2024-2026) (papers.md; coverage_report.md; taxonomy_draft.md).
- Strong system share (~37% OSDI/SOSP/FAST/EuroSys) coexists with kernel/algorithm work; no single technique dominates >20% — distribution is disaggregation+scheduling ~18% each, eviction 10.5%, compression 15.8% (coverage_report.md; literature_matrix.md).
- Central organizing tension is trading GPU HBM capacity (KV 1.2-3 TB at batch 512 for 175B, 540B PaLM) against bandwidth (PCIe 2-64 GB/s vs HBM 2 TB/s, IB 200-400 Gbps, CXL 750 ns/64B), interference (compute-bound prefill vs memory-bound decode), and reuse hit rate (taxonomy_draft.md; bottleneck_map.md B1/B7/B6; assumption_map.md A1/A2).

# 2. Major Research Families

- **KV allocation & paged memory management:** Fixed-size blocks (vLLM B=16, SGLang RadixAttention per-node paged tensors, LMCache 256-token chunks + 1 MB DMA coalescing, FlexGen LP placement) bounding fragmentation waste <=1 block and enabling CoW sharing (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.1; bottleneck_map.md B2; literature_matrix.md).
- **Eviction & semantic/layer-adaptive compression:** Heavy-hitter/submodular (H2O), attention sinks + window (StreamingLLM), persistence (Scissorhands), observation-window voting (SnapKV), pyramidal funneling (PyramidKV), semantic chunk (ChunkKV), landmark low-rank (ShadowKV), phase-aware prefill/decode split (SCOPE) — all target 5-20% budget with sparsity-driven selection (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.2-3; bottleneck_map.md B12/B14; contradictions.md T4).
- **Quantization & composite compression:** Asymmetric per-channel key / per-token value 2-bit with residual (KIVI), quant+low-rank+sparse (GEAR rank 4/2 + s=2%), delta bitstream codec (CacheGen) — 2.6x peak memory to 3.5-4.3x bandwidth reduction, orthogonal to eviction (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.4; bottleneck_map.md B14; contradictions.md T5).
- **Prefix caching, KV sharing & selective recomputation:** Radix tree exact reuse (SGLang), knowledge tree + PGDSF (RAGCache), CoW (vLLM), any-position HKVD 15% recompute with RoPE re-encoding (CacheBlend), CCI/CFO-filtered partial prefill (Cache-Craft), position-independent pre-RoPE + trainable link tokens (KVLink) forming a maturity ladder prefix -> arbitrary -> PIC (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.5-6,8; bottleneck_map.md B3/B4; contradictions.md T6).
- **Offloading & hierarchical memory:** LP offload GPU/CPU/disk (FlexGen), rehearsal speculative prefetch <10% (InfiniGen), value offload + low-rank key (ShadowKV), DRAM->SSD async hierarchy (CachedAttention), CXL switched pool 8 TB @1 TB/s DAX mmap (Beluga), formal GH200 placement search (DynamicPlacement), disk cross-instance sharing (Shared RAG-DCache) (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.7; bottleneck_map.md B5/B13; contradictions.md T7).
- **KV transfer & communication optimization:** Disaggregated pool + GPUDirect RDMA Messenger (Mooncake), shape reshape (L,2,B,H)->(B,L,2,H) + segment coalescing 23469->1 NCCL calls (FlowKV), NIXL P2P + SMC spread (Dynamo/LMCache), delta codec (CacheGen) — transfer 0.944s->0.053s (-96%) when coalesced (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.9; bottleneck_map.md B6; evaluation_map.md).
- **Scheduling, batching & routing:** Iteration-level/selective batching (Orca), chunked-prefill + token-budget stall-free (Sarathi-Serve tau 512/2048), token preemption MLFQ (FastServe), hotness-aware admission (HotPrefix), online theory (OnlineScheduling), live migration virtual-usage (Llumnix), KV-aware routing (Mooncake Conductor, Dynamo Smart Router, FlowKV Global Controller) (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.10-11; bottleneck_map.md B7/B8/B10; evaluation_map.md).
- **Prefill/decode disaggregation & distributed elasticity:** Heterogeneous pools prompt/token/mixed (Splitwise), goodput-optimized placement Alg1/2 (DistServe), streaming fault-tolerant (DejaVu), elastic sequence parallelism dynamic DoP (LoongServe), multi-round adaptive routing (AMPD), industrial runtime with autoscaling (Dynamo) (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.12-13; bottleneck_map.md B7; contradictions.md T1/T2).
- **Workload-aware specialization:** RAG order-sensitive reuse (RAGCache/CacheBlend/Cache-Craft), agent Step Graph + TTL retention (KVFlow/Continuum), long-context ESP/landmarks (LoongServe/ShadowKV), heterogeneous CXL-aware placement (Beluga/DynamicPlacement) — fastest growth 2024-2026 (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.14; stage1_summary.md).

# 3. Major Bottlenecks

- **GPU HBM capacity exhaustion:** KV dominates weights (OPT-175B batch512x2048 1.2-3.8x, PaLM 540B 3 TB, Llama2-7B 100K >50 GB, 1M OOM at batch 2) capping batch and effective usage 20-38% vs 96% with paging (bottleneck_map.md B1; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.1; assumption_map.md A1).
- **Memory fragmentation & reservation waste:** Variable-length contiguous max-token reservation leaves 60-80% waste; paged indirection adds 20-26% kernel overhead; small pages (62.5 KB/16 tokens) achieve 4 GBps vs 46 GBps at 1 MB due to PCIe/NCCL fragmentation (bottleneck_map.md B2; evaluation_map.md).
- **Cache miss / low hit rate:** Strict prefix hit 8% requests /18% tokens, max reuse plateaus 50% even at infinite storage, 50% blocks never reused vs hot blocks >10k accesses; order sensitivity breaks naive reuse (bottleneck_map.md B3; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.5-6; coverage_report.md W1).
- **Redundant recomputation:** Same chunks reappear in 60%+ RAG requests (top 5% docs ->60% hits) causing 12B tokens/month ~9600 GPU-hours waste; 4000-token prefill 3s/6s on A40 34B/70B if not reused (bottleneck_map.md B4; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.8).
- **PCIe bandwidth saturation:** CPU->GPU fetch over PCIe 2 GB/s (A10G Gen1) to 64 GB/s (H100 Gen5) is 10-100x lower than HBM 2 TB/s; 75% of 10.55 us transfer is sync overhead, small 64 KB transfers only 4 GBps (bottleneck_map.md B5; evaluation_map.md).
- **Network transfer overhead (disaggregated):** 1.13 GB per 512 tokens on 66B ->90 Gbps at 10 rps; block-wise PagedAttention causes 23k NCCL calls per request, 25% of E2E at 13K tokens before coalescing, <0.1% after co-location/RDMA (bottleneck_map.md B6; contradictions.md T3; literature_matrix.md).
- **Prefill/decode interference:** Colocated batch mixes compute-bound prefill (flat after 512 tokens) and memory-bound decode causing stalls seconds, pipeline bubbles ~950 ms for Falcon-180B 4K, TBT 28.3x vs decode-only (bottleneck_map.md B7; assumption_map.md A2; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.12).
- **Scheduler inefficiency & queueing:** FCFS request-level batching blocks late arrivals; iteration-level still needs manual max_bs and SLO awareness; competitive ratio Omega(sqrt(n)) hardness for KV-constrained online scheduling (bottleneck_map.md B8; evaluation_map.md).
- **Poor reuse prediction & hotness misclassification:** LRU evicts soon-to-reuse agents while retaining unlikely suffixes (KVFlow Expresser miss at T=14); burst alternation drives hit 0% vs optimal 66% (bottleneck_map.md B9; assumption_map.md A4).
- **Load imbalance & autoscaling inefficiency:** KV-aware routing concentrates hot blocks creating hotspots; P/D ratio mismatch (2P+2D worse than 3P+1D) and skewed prefix load need replication threshold tuning (bottleneck_map.md B10; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.11).
- **Tail latency SLO violations:** Near capacity, TBT tail spikes, pipeline jitter and transfer queuing cause only 57% meet TBT vs ~100% with proper disaggregation; SLOs defined as 10x TTFT /5x TBT (bottleneck_map.md B11; evaluation_map.md).
- **Long-context memory pressure (quadratic):** O(n^2) attention for 1K-1M variance forces >50 GB per request, heavy-hitter drift during decoding, OOM at 33K vanilla vs 380K with compression (bottleneck_map.md B12; stage1_summary.md).
- **Hierarchical tiering complexity:** HBM 2 TB/s vs DRAM 50 GB/s vs PCIe/CXL vs RDMA vs S3 1 GBps with differing coherence (CXL2 non-coherent needs ntstore+CLFLUSH/UC) and placement decisions per LP (bottleneck_map.md B13; assumption_map.md A11).
- **Compression-vs-quality degradation:** Aggressive eviction/quant (0.7% budget, 2-bit) causes 35% drop or MQA collapse (Falcon 2-bit 59.8->57.4), error compounds autoregressively and is task-dependent (CoT vs retrieval) (bottleneck_map.md B14; limitation_map.md L13).

# 4. Dominant Assumptions

- **HBM capacity is throughput bottleneck:** KV memory caps batch and linear throughput claims rely on it; fails when compute-bound plateau (e.g., vLLM Alpaca) or H100 FLOPS/bandwidth divergence 3.43x vs 1.64x (assumption_map.md A1; bottleneck_map.md B1).
- **Prefill compute-bound vs decode memory-bound dichotomy is stable:** Justifies heterogeneous pools and chunked budgets; fragile under MoE, speculative decoding, MLA or GQA/FlashAttention-3 shifts and heavy+heavy corners (assumption_map.md A2; contradictions.md T1).
- **Requests independent with no cross-request sharing beyond reserved prefixes:** Simplifies block tables and queuing (M/D/1 Poisson) but invalidated by production RAG skew (top 3% 60% hits, 94% cross-user hit) and SGLang 50-99% prefix opportunity (assumption_map.md A3; coverage_report.md; stage1_summary.md).
- **LRU / recency approximates reuse (temporal locality):** Underpins RadixAttention leaf-first eviction; contested by PGDSF (+2-75% over LRU), Cuckoo hotness, steps-to-execution and TTL (2.19x over LRU) (assumption_map.md A4; bottleneck_map.md B9).
- **Interconnect bandwidth homogeneous and transfer hideable via overlap:** Disaggregation <0.1% / <7% claims assume NVLink 600 GB/s / IB 400 Gbps and layer-wise async; breaks under heterogeneous H100->A100 IB gaps, PCIe 3.0 16 GB/s, RDMA sglist 30 limit and 75% sync overhead (assumption_map.md A5; contradictions.md T3; bottleneck_map.md B6).
- **Workload arrivals/lengths stationary Poisson, fit once over hours/days:** Enables offline C1-C5 profiling and placement search; poor for bursty long-tail (10% slowest tool 52-94% delay, HiCache 0.57x under 64 concurrent) (assumption_map.md A6; evaluation_map.md; bottleneck_map.md B8).
- **Future length (output tokens) unknown at schedule time (no clairvoyance):** Justifies FCFS/MLFQ over SJF/SRPT; attacked by 74.9% granularity-200 predictors but still limited (assumption_map.md A7; literature_matrix.md TetriInfer).
- **Recomputation/fetch cost uniform linear in tokens:** Enables token-budget tau and linear T(l,u); violated by tile quantization 257->32% slower, sparsity 84.3%, outlier channels (assumption_map.md A8; bottleneck_map.md B14).
- **Attention sparse power-law with persistent heavy hitters:** Supports fixed 20% budget (H2O 95% sparsity) and funneling; weak for dense copy/needle-dispersed tasks where retrieval drops 35% at 20% budget (assumption_map.md A9; contradictions.md T4).
- **Initial tokens are attention sinks pinned for streaming:** 4 sinks restore PPL 5158->5.40 to 4M stable; varies (1 sink suffices Falcon/MPT, learnable sink reduces need) (assumption_map.md A10; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.2).
- **Two-tier HBM+DRAM hierarchy sufficient:** Simplifies LP models but invalidated by 1M contexts needing SSD/CXL pool 8 TB @1 TB/s, 750 ns (Beluga) and enterprise hierarchical DRAM-SSD-S3 (assumption_map.md A11; bottleneck_map.md B13; coverage_report.md W2).
- **Token importance decidable from local observation window / instruction tokens alpha~8:** Last window proxies future generation; fails when question at front or whole-document summarization, instruction position dependent (assumption_map.md A12; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.2-3).

# 5. Evaluation Norms

- **Standard throughput/latency metrics:** TTFT (prefill), TPOT/TBT/ITL (decode), Throughput/Goodput/Capacity (tokens/s, RPS/GPU under SLO), E2E normalized latency, P50/P90/P99 tails — reported in 15+/24 papers as community standard; compression papers mandate added quality dimension (evaluation_map.md Sec.1; literature_matrix.md Sec.C).
- **SLO definitions:** Goodput = RPS meeting 90%/99% TTFT+TPOT; Sarathi capacity = max RPS with median scheduling delay <2s + P99 TBT; Mooncake TTFT_P90 <=10x baseline, TBT_P90 <=5x; strict 0.1s/1s vs relaxed 1-15s budgets (evaluation_map.md Sec.1; bottleneck_map.md B11).
- **Memory/hit/bandwidth metrics:** Peak memory & KV bytes/token (LLaMA-65B 2.5 MB, 70B GQA 0.31 MB), fragmentation effective usage (20% ->96%), hit rate CachedLength/ComputationLength, bandwidth/transfer s/GB/s (NCCL calls 23469->1, RDMA 87 GB/s), GPU util/MFU and quality (F1/Rouge/PPL/exact match, Needle, RULER, LongBench, SWE-Bench) (evaluation_map.md Sec.1; bottleneck_map.md).
- **Dominant baselines:** vLLM PagedAttention de-facto universal (15/24 reviewed, >=60%) pinned to v0.2.5-v0.10.2; SGLang/RadixAttention for prefix/RAG/agent (6/24); Mooncake/Dynamo/LMCache for disaggregated (5/24); Splitwise/DistServe/TetriInfer for P/D; FlexGen/DeepSpeed for offload; InfiniGen/ShadowKV etc. for compression (evaluation_map.md Sec.2; literature_matrix.md).
- **Workload strata:** Synthetic controlled Poisson (U(32,512) in, U(1,128) out) for sweeps; ShareGPT (most reused, heavy-tail, 1730 median/7059 arxiv) for chat; conversational multi-turn ShareGPT 9K/52K turns; long-context PG19/ LongBench /RULER/NIAH 16K-1M; RAG Wikipedia 0.3M/top-k 2-6 skewed 20x; agent ReAct 10-agent 8192/32/32 with tool gaps 0.9-1.9s; prefix-heavy 5-20-shot; bursty high-concurrency 64 concurrent Gamma CV (evaluation_map.md Sec.3; stage1_summary.md).
- **Hardware skew and limited scale:** Single-GPU A10/A40/A6000 dominates eviction/compression; multi-node 32 GPUs (DistServe 4x8 A100), 20 nodes/160 GPUs (Mooncake A800), 16 instances/2 servers CXL (Beluga H20); interconnect NVLink 600 GB/s intra vs 25-50 Gbps inter vs PCIe Gen1 2 GB/s to Gen5 64 GB/s; precision often FP16/BF16 or [NOT REPORTED]; many claims extrapolate via simulator (<3% MAPE) or dummy models not at 1M (evaluation_map.md Sec.4; limitation_map.md L3).

# 6. Mature Areas

- **Paged KV allocation & block management:** vLLM 16-token blocks bounding waste to <=1 block with CoW, widely adopted as open-source base (2-4x over Orca, 20-38% ->96% effective) and reproduced in SGLang/LMCache/Dynamo — many papers, years of production, de-facto standard (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.1 Maturity=Mature; limitation_map.md L2; evaluation_map.md).
- **Continuous/iteration-level batching & stall-free hybrid scheduling:** Orca iteration-level selective batching (36.9x) evolved to Sarathi-Serve chunked-prefill 512 + piggyback budget (2.6-5.6x) now vLLM default; adoption in production mitigates prefill-decode stalls within colocated pools (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.10 Maturity=Mature; stage1_summary.md; literature_matrix.md).
- **Exact prefix caching (automatic sharing):** RadixAttention tree with LRU leaf-first, ref-count, longest-prefix DFS (50-99% hit, 96% optimal, production 52-74%, overhead <0.3%) standardized in SGLang/ vLLM prefix caching/LMCache enterprise replication — single-tier exact match at scale (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.5 Maturity=Mature; bottleneck_map.md B3; coverage_report.md Well Covered #3).
- **Serving foundation distributed baseline:** Tensor + pipeline parallelism for 13B-341B (Orca 1-32 A100) and goodput-aware disaggregation placement as baseline for newer elastic work — foundational but superseded for large contexts (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.13 partially mature; stage1_summary.md).
- **System-level standardization:** LMCache unified KV layer (256-token chunks, 1 MB coalescing 4->46 GBps, connector abstraction) and Dynamo NIXL runtime demonstrate integration of paging+prefix+offload, indicating preconditions for maturity are satisfied (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.1/5; stage1_summary.md Important Systems).

# 7. Active Areas

- **Token eviction & budget allocation:** H2O/Scissorhands/StreamingLLM -> SnapKV/PyramidKV/SCOPE pipeline with per-layer/head adaptive allocation, observation-window filters and phase separation; 3-5 papers each 2023-2025, strong gains (5-20x compression) but heuristic budgets (alpha=20, 15-30% r*) still tuned manually (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.2-3 Active; limitation_map.md L1; bottleneck_map.md B14).
- **Quantization + composite compression:** KIVI asymmetric 2-bit (G=32,R=128) -> GEAR low-rank+sparse -> CacheGen delta codec; 4-bit mature, 2-bit transitioning active->mature for NLU but needs compensation for CoT (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.4 Active; contradictions.md T5).
- **KV sharing arbitrary-position & recomputation fusion:** Any-position HKVD selective recompute (CacheBlend), contamination-aware partial prefill (Cache-Craft), pre-RoPE storage + global RoPE (KVLink) — training-free vs trainable (5 link tokens) tradeoff, 2024-2025 burst, fast-growing but not default (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.6 Emerging->Active; bottleneck_map.md B4; coverage_report.md Well Covered #3).
- **KV transfer / communication optimization:** FlowKV shape reshape + segment coalescing (-96%), Mooncake Messenger + CPP, NIXL zero-copy — rapid industry uptake (Dynamo, LMCache) but threshold tuning and heterogeneous coupling remain (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.9 Active; bottleneck_map.md B6; contradictions.md T3).
- **Prefill/decode disaggregation & heterogeneous placement:** Splitwise HA 2.35x iso-cost/power, DistServe goodput search 2.0-4.6x (12.6x tighter), TetriInfer chunked+predictive — active 2023-2024 seminal, production validated but double weight memory cost remains (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.12 Active; stage1_summary.md).
- **RAG non-prefix reuse & ranking-aware caching:** Knowledge tree PGDSF -> selective recompute/CFO pipeline integrating retrieval skew (20x) and order sensitivity; moving from emerging to active with enterprise metrics (51% redundant cut, 1.6x throughput) (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.14 RAG-aware; bottleneck_map.md B9).
- **Agent/workflow-aware scheduling (early active):** Step Graph prediction, TTL retention starting to show 2-8x JCT gains, still transitioning from prototypes (KVFlow 2.19x, Continuum 8x) toward active adoption (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.14; limitation_map.md L12).

# 8. Sparse / Underexplored Areas

- **Theoretical online scheduling under KV cache constraints:** Only Online Scheduling 2025 provides competitive ratio for KV-constrained batch scheduling (Omega(sqrt(n)) hardness) — single proof-of-concept, no multi-worker heterogeneous extension; underexplored (taxonomy_draft.md Maturity Sparse; limitation_map.md L12; evaluation_map.md Sec.4 [NOT REPORTED]).
- **Formal heterogeneous placement beyond GH200:** Dynamic Placement GH200 model (min sum max with SA over W,R, 5.87x upper bound) assumes perfect future knowledge and single GPU type; not online nor evaluated on PCIe5/CXL3 beyond GH200 — underexplored (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.14 Heterogeneous; assumption_map.md A11; bottleneck_map.md B13).
- **Disk cross-instance persistent cache:** Shared RAG-DCache (disk queuing-window pre-generation +15-71%) and CachedAttention DRAM->SSD (87% TTFT, 7.8x prefill) are 1-2 papers with 2-GPU or 4xA100 single-node validation; cost/power vs adding GPUs, crash consistency and auto-tiering DRAM-SSD-S3 not formalized — underexplored (taxonomy_draft.md Cat.7/14 Sparse; bottleneck_map.md B13; limitation_map.md L11).
- **Fault tolerance, consistency & availability for disaggregated pools:** Most disaggregated pools (Splitwise, DistServe, Mooncake, LMCache) lack checkpointing/WAL/replication; DejaVu token-level streaming replication is isolated and not adopted; RDMA incast and 100s-node Controller bottleneck not measured — underexplored (limitation_map.md L11; bottleneck_map.md Evidence Gaps; taxonomy_draft.md).
- **Multi-tenant isolation, privacy & security for KV reuse:** CoW sharing, radix forests, hash dedup (512-token blocks) enable timing side-channels (3.9x hit vs miss) and cross-user doc reconstruction (94% cross-user hit reported); no per-tenant permission or isolation evaluated — underexplored (limitation_map.md L7; bottleneck_map.md Evidence Gaps).
- **End-to-end cost/power/energy TCO:** Perf/$ claims use rental prices ($17.6 A100 vs $38 H100, $0.0088/GB DRAM) not provisioned power (700W vs 400W) + PUE + network + replication overhead; double weight memory for P/D pools not quantified; few joules/token measurements — underexplored (limitation_map.md L8; evaluation_map.md Sec.1 Cost; bottleneck_map.md).
- **Joint multi-axis optimization:** No paper jointly optimizes token budget * bit-width * prefix policy * placement; stacking (paging + eviction + quant + disaggregation) via connectors is demonstrated but Pareto not explored and per-layer G/R/rank uniform heuristics remain — underexplored (taxonomy_draft.md Cross-Cutting Synergy; limitation_map.md L2; stage1_summary.md Stage 2 Readiness #3).
- **Production multi-round state lifetimes beyond single-turn:** Multi-turn TTL/Step Graph cover linear ReAct traces; branching/loops, elastic ESP token-granular pool, and joint prefill+decode drift correction at enterprise scale are sparsely validated — underexplored (limitation_map.md L12; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.14 Agent; coverage_report.md W2 before supplement).

# 9. Repeated Limitations

- **Static manually-tuned budgets/thresholds:** PyramidKV alpha=20/beta, KIVI G=32/R=128, GEAR rank 4/2 s=2%, SCOPE Top-K+alpha, Sarathi tau 512/2048, CacheBlend r*=15%, HotPrefix admission 10, Mooncake kvcache_balancing_threshold — widely acknowledged across >=7 papers, no online adaptive tuning proven (limitation_map.md L1; assumption_map.md Cross-Cutting; bottleneck_map.md Evidence Gaps).
- **No joint optimization across orthogonal axes:** Token eviction vs bit-quant vs prefix reuse vs disaggregation studied in isolation; gear noted orthogonal to quantization, DistServe/Splitwise deferred chunked vs disagg comparison, LMCache cache vs queue not auto-decided (limitation_map.md L2; taxonomy_draft.md Synergy Map).
- **Narrow hardware & small-cluster validation:** Single-GPU families (A10/A40/A6000/T4/V100) and <=32 GPUs, even 160 GPUs dummy model; no H100/GB200 NVL72 heterogeneity, CXL beyond rack prototype, PCIe5 vs NVLink variability; simulator substitutes for TCO (limitation_map.md L3; evaluation_map.md Highlight table 14 papers with limited conditions).
- **Hit-rate vs fairness/starvation trade-off:** Longest-prefix-first is DFS-optimal but starves small hot prefixes; hit-optimal routing creates hotspots vs P99 TBT; no weighted fair queuing with prefix affinity integrated with SLOs (limitation_map.md L4; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.11; bottleneck_map.md B10).
- **KV transfer heterogeneity assumptions unrealistic:** Assumed IB H100->A100 feasible, 25 Gbps vs 800 Gbps, SGList 30 vs 128 chunks, NCCL fragmentation validated as 25% but reshape coupled to PagedAttention; cross-node 1M context via PB offload not evaluated (limitation_map.md L5; contradictions.md T3; bottleneck_map.md B6).
- **No true context window extension:** Window/sink methods stabilize recent coherence (4M) but discard middle tokens; Needle/RULER gains at 8-32K hide collapse on 128K reasoning where H2O 9.33 vs ShadowKV 86.88 full (limitation_map.md L6; bottleneck_map.md B12).
- **Cross-request reuse without isolation risks leakage:** Timing side channels and hash-based dedup across tenants enable doc reconstruction; enterprise 94% cross-user hit rate assumes public knowledge (limitation_map.md L7; assumption_map.md A3).
- **Dollar cost / power / energy not measured:** Provisioned vs dynamic power, DRAM/SSD rental, RDMA NIC vs CXL adapter economics not stable; claimed 2.35x same-cost or 70% saving would shrink with full TCO (limitation_map.md L8; evaluation_map.md).
- **Synthetic / non-representative workloads mask SLO interactions:** Poisson, 1-hour traces, 6000 GPT-4 synthetic queries, uniform 512-token chunks, 200-query samples vs bursty multi-tenant heavy-tail skew; benchmark 96% optimal vs 52-74% production hit shows gap (limitation_map.md L9; evaluation_map.md).
- **Positional encoding coupling breaks naive reuse:** RoPE before/after, cache-relative re-encoding, PagedAttention block table interaction require per-method correction; misapplied naive PPL >1e3 vs 5.40 corrected (limitation_map.md L10; assumption_map.md A12).
- **No fault-tolerant hierarchical persistence:** No synchronous replication/WAL for CPU/DRAM/SSD KV; failure during layer-wise transfer wastes entire prefill; overload prediction based on uniform td fails upon node failure (limitation_map.md L11; bottleneck_map.md B13).
- **Multi-turn / agentic lifetimes not captured by single-turn assumptions:** LRU leaf-first and session all-or-nothing waste suffixes; tool gaps seconds-minutes (avg 0.9-1.9s, turns 6-11, 70K tokens/program) need TTL/Step Graph max/min not single-round goodput (limitation_map.md L12; bottleneck_map.md B9).
- **Compression brittleness is task-dependent (weakly evidenced):** Extreme 0.7% or 2-bit collapse on GSM8K CoT (95% drop) vs near-lossless on retrieval summarization due to dense correlated context; fixed chunks vs semantic chunk ratio changes; weakly evidenced across limited 1M suite (limitation_map.md L13).

# 10. Interesting Tensions

- **Disaggregation (separate pools + KV transfer) vs chunked-prefill hybrid (single pool + token budget):** Splitwise 2.35x iso-cost/power and DistServe 2.0-4.6x goodput (12.6x tighter SLO) claim separate is optimal vs Sarathi 2.6-5.6x capacity via stall-free tau=512/2048 without migration; resolution: metric (goodput under strict SLO vs capacity), workload (coding 1500/13 vs arxiv 7059) and hardware (IB 400 Gbps with MSCCL++ zero-copy vs 25 Gbps limited) condition dominance — Pareto-optimal at different points (contradictions.md T1; bottleneck_map.md B7).
- **Static optimal partition vs elastic sequence parallelism:** DistServe static 4P+4D minutes search optimal at <=32K (4.3x lower TTFT) vs LoongServe ESP zero-overhead scale-down + multi-master decode 5.81x over DistServe and 3.85x over chunked on same 8xA800 when 1M variance (105.97x prefill variance, 488 GB at 1M) exceeds any fixed group memory (contradictions.md T2; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.13).
- **KV transfer negligible (<0.1% total, constant 5-8 ms, 0.8% E2E) vs dominant (25% E2E, 23469 NCCL calls):** Both true conditional on implementation: naive PagedAttention NCCL per-layer per-block fragmentation is significant (FlowKV 96.8% reduction) vs optimized layer-wise async MSCCL++ one-sided put with reshape+segment alignment+NVLink co-location/RDMA Messenger makes it negligible (contradictions.md T3; bottleneck_map.md B6; evaluation_map.md).
- **Persistent uniform eviction (20% budget, sinks=4, heavy-hitter submodular) vs pyramidal/phase-separated:** H2O 5x near-lossless 20% vs PyramidKV 12% matches full via pyramidal funneling (lower broad upper concentrated) vs SnapKV 92% prefill pruning vs SCOPE 20% prefill-only collapse on GSM8K+ 95% but not retrieval; resolution: workload phase — retrieval tolerates prefill-only uniform, reasoning needs phase-separated decode budget, streaming needs sinks (contradictions.md T4; taxonomy_draft.md Cat.2-3; assumption_map.md A9/A12).
