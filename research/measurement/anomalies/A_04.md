# Observation

Arrival pattern has limited effect on throughput/latency except for queue depth.

Synthetic 512/64, conc8, 40 requests, closed vs poisson/bursty/gamma/uniform at rates 2,4,8:

- **Closed (baseline):** thr 0.897±0.006 rps, p50 5.68±0.19s, p95 8.78±0.13s, queue mean 20.22s (repro 3×, 2026-08-28 09:20)
- **Bursty r2:** thr 0.887±0.018 (-1.1% vs closed), p50 5.63±0.33s (-0.8%), p95 8.84±0.22 (+0.7%), **queue mean 6.89s (-66% vs closed)** — queue much lower but latency and throughput almost identical.
- **Poisson r2:** thr 0.887±0.018 (-1.1%), p50 5.74s (+1%), queue 14–18s (-30% vs closed) — similar to closed.
- **At rate 8:** closed thr0.897, bursty thr0.887 (-1.1%), p50 5.67 vs 5.63 (-0.7%) — no difference. Single-run coarse showed bursty r8 thr1.012 (+12% vs closed 0.905) and p50 4.84 vs 5.72 (-15%) but repro with 3× shows this was noise (single-run outlier).
- **Poisson/gamma/uniform at 2,4,8:** thr within ±2% of closed, p50 within ±3%, p95 within ±3% — flat.

In short: for conc8 saturated server, open-loop arrival (poisson/gamma/uniform/bursty) vs closed yields <2% throughput change and <4% latency change, despite queue mean varying 66% (bursty low rate). Bursty reduces client-side queue but not end-to-end latency — latency is dominated by server-side queuing, not arrival pacing, at this concurrency.

Coarse single-run hinted at bursty r2 p50 3.94 vs closed 5.72 (-31%) and queue 3.77 vs 20.09 (-81%), but 3× repro shows p50 effect disappears (5.63 vs 5.68) while queue effect remains.

# Reproducibility

- **Initial coarse single:** bursty r2 showed -31% p50, but only 1 run per cell, no variance estimate — flagged as candidate.
- **Independent repro 3× (fresh process, fresh seeds 7000+rep, same locked config):** p50 5.67 vs 5.63 difference 0.8% < CV 5.9% — **NOT REPRODUCED for latency**. Thr difference 1.1% < CV 2% — not reproduced. Queue mean -66% with large variance (6.89±3.94, CV 57%) — queue reduction is directionally stable but latency not.
- **Other distributions:** poisson/gamma/uniform at all rates show <2% thr and <3% p50 vs closed across coarse 12 points — reproduced as flat (no effect).
- **Raw:** `sweeps/raw/sweepE_arrival_*.json` + `reproA5_*.json`, 3 reps, environment_hash 6c78dce7b294, queue_time per-request in CSV.

**Gate:** **NOT REPRODUCED for latency/throughput** (only queue). Downgraded to PROBABLY VALID for queue effect, INVALID for latency tail claim.

# Baseline Comparison

- **Closed vs open at same concurrency:** closed is the baseline (all arrival_offset 0). Open-loop with rate 2–8 and conc8 saturated should be similar to closed because server queue dominates — observed flat confirms.
- **Burstiness expectation (literature):** bursty should increase tail due to transient overload; observed queue lower for bursty r2, opposite — suggests closed loop already bursty via `Semaphore` queuing, so arrival burstiness is masked.

---

# Reality Check

- **Realistic:** YES. Poisson/bursty arrivals are realistic open-loop patterns (user traffic). Rate 2 rps is mild overload vs thr 0.9 rps, rate 8 is heavy burst.
- **Plausible:** At high conc8, server is saturated (thr flat), so arrival pacing matters less than service time — plausible that latency is server-queue dominated.
- **Stress-test:** Rate 8 with 40 requests in open-loop may overflow timeout; but all runs succeeded 38/38.
- **Synthetic-only risk:** Moderate — bursty parameters (burst_rate 3×, idle 0.3×) are synthetic; real trace burstiness may differ.

# Preliminary Importance

**Low (downgraded from Medium after repro)**

- Throughput/latency effect is not reproducible (1.1% vs noise 2–6% = artifact), so not a research-worthy anomaly.
- Queue reduction (-66%) is reproducible but does not translate to end-to-end latency on this engine — suggests client-side queue vs server-side queue separation, but harness queue_time is client-side and not a system bottleneck metric.
- For Stage 3R-C, arrival pattern can be deprioritized for this engine/concurrency range; focus instead on concurrency and output length which dominate.
