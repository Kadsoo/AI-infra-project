# Research Question

Quote RQ-8 verbatim from `knowledge/stage3_candidates.md` (lines 152-194):

> **RQ-8 — Do retained-token count, KV bit-width, and storage tier interact non-additively?**
>
> **Observation:** The corpus contains hybrid reductions already: H2O combines retention with low-bit KV storage, GEAR combines quantization with low-rank/sparse compensation, and ShadowKV combines low-rank state with offload. It therefore does **not** support the claim that every paper optimizes one independent axis. What remains unmeasured in the supplied corpus is whether retained-token count, bit-width, and tier placement compose additively under the same workload and SLO.
>
> **Fragile Assumption / Limitation:** Stacked optimizations are treated as approximately orthogonal: reducing bytes should help placement, and reducing tokens should reduce transfer/attention cost. Kernel granularity, residual buffers, approximation error, and fetch latency can invalidate that assumption.
>
> **Failure Scenario:** Aggressive token retention combined with low-bit KV and a cold tier, especially for a reasoning/RAG workload where quality repair or small transfers dominate.
>
> **Observable Consequence:** (a) A factorial interaction term is non-zero for TTFT, TPOT, memory, or quality; (b) Pareto ordering reverses: the best single-axis setting is not part of the best combined configuration; (c) The combined gain is sub-additive or causes a quality/tail regression.

# Motivation

Stage 2B's audit explicitly rejected the "gains should multiply" claim (`stage2_final_review.md` §3, row "No joint optimization exists"): H2O already combines retention with low-bit storage, GEAR stacks quantization with low-rank and sparse compensation, and ShadowKV stacks low-rank keys with value offload. The surviving question (G1 audit → RQ-8, classified **C — benchmark/evaluation gap**, §6) is a **measurement** question about existing mechanisms, not an invention question. The audit's §7 item 3 retains "non-additivity of token reduction, numerical precision, and tier choice" as a falsifiable research-space item, and §9 states the largest uncertainty is whether interactions survive a **matched workload × hardware/topology × metric** experiment. This hypothesis is that experiment, nothing more.

Each axis is individually validated on different models, hardware, metrics, and quality targets, which makes cross-paper multiplication invalid: H2O reports 29×/3× throughput on T4/A100 with OPT-6.7B/30B at 20% retention (5× memory reduction); KIVI reports 2.35–3.47× throughput and 2.6× peak-memory reduction with Llama-2/Falcon/Mistral at 2-bit, hardware largely unreported; GEAR reports 2.1–5.07× on a V100 16GB with Llama-2/3/Mistral at 2-bit with low-rank+sparse repair (batch 3→18, peak memory 2.39×); ShadowKV reports up to 3.04× throughput and 6× batch on an A100 with Llama-3/GLM-4/Yi at 128K–1M context using low-rank keys + value offload; FlexGen reports up to 100× on a T4 with OPT-175B across a three-tier GPU+CPU+Disk hierarchy; InfiniGen reports up to 3.00× over FlexGen on an RTX A6000 via selective prefetch. None of these multipliers is portable: different models, GPUs, sequence lengths (2K vs 1M), metric definitions, and quality benchmarks. `evaluation_map.md` and `contradictions.md` already condition every such comparison on workload/hardware/metric; RQ-8 simply requires the same conditioning *within one factorial matrix*.

The individual axes are already known to be condition-dependent, which is a precondition for interaction. KIVI's 2-bit is near-lossless on most LongBench tasks (Llama-2-7B avg 44.27 vs 44.52) but needs the R=128 full-precision residual window to survive GSM8K (KIVI-2 20.77 vs fake-2bit 12.21), and Falcon-7B (MQA) needs 4-bit, not 2-bit. GEAR's premise is that quantization error is concentrated and task-dependent: plain 2-bit per-token collapses to 7.67 average on 8-shot CoT vs 40.20 with low-rank+sparse repair. H2O's heavy-hitter assumption (A9) is weak for retrieval-dense and reasoning workloads. If quantization error is concentrated on a task-dependent token subset, then **which tokens eviction retains determines how much bit-width error survives** — the two axes cannot be assumed independent a priori (T1, T12; KIVI/PyramidKV explicitly motivate token×bit combination in their notes).

Tier behavior is likewise a function of what the other two axes produce. FlexGen's cost model is `T = max(I/O, compute)`: placement benefit is exactly transfer volume = (retained tokens × bytes per token). InfiniGen shows that without selective fetch, transfer dominates (96.9% of per-block time in the FlexGen baseline) and that <10% fetched KV suffices — i.e., the tier's benefit depends on how little state must move. ShadowKV's equivalent-bandwidth claim (7.2 TB/s, 3.6× A100) assumes PCIe 31.5 GB/s and a 60% temporal hit rate; it explicitly lists quantization as "orthogonal... not integrated." H2O's own throughput tables show offload frequency changes with retention (OPT-30B on T4: FlexGen 144,C vs H2O 728,C). Whether byte savings translate into tier-latency savings depends on transfer granularity, chunk layout, and concurrency (T5, A8, A11) — precisely the unmeasured link.

The minimal scientific step is therefore a 3×2×2 factorial (retention × bit-width × tier) plus a workload factor on a matched harness, with interaction terms estimated directly. This is the only design that discriminates additive composition (null) from non-additivity, and it reuses only existing mechanisms — no universal controller, no joint optimizer, no new algorithm, per the Stage 2B "do not promote" list. A null result is a legitimate and useful outcome: it reclassifies RQ-8 as engineering composition (`stage3_candidates.md`: "A null result is also valuable"). The failure scenario that motivates the question — aggressive retention + 2-bit + cold tier under reasoning/RAG — is treated as the corner where an interaction, if real, should be largest; the hypothesis does not presume it is real.

# Primary Hypothesis

Under a matched single-7B serving harness with fixed SLO, fixed quality tolerance, and identical kernels/workloads across all cells, stacking the three existing mechanisms — retained-token budget (100%/60%/20%), KV bit-width (16-bit/2-bit), and storage tier (GPU-only / GPU+host) — causes the two-way and three-way factorial interaction terms to be non-zero and to explain a material share of the main-effect variance: support requires a significant interaction term (α=0.05, BH-corrected) with R²_int_rel = SS_interactions/SS_main_effects > 0.50 in at least one of TTFT, TPOT, throughput, HBM usage, or quality across the workload matrix, compared with the baseline of strictly additive main-effect composition. (Statistics unified per revision R2 — support and falsification now share the same denominator; ANOVA on cell means, not per-request data.)

Negation is explicit: if every interaction term is statistically non-significant (α=0.05) and R²_int_rel ≤ 0.10 for all metrics and both workloads, the composition is additive and the hypothesis is falsified (see Falsification Condition).

# Sub-Hypotheses

**H1 — retention × bit-width interaction (quality).** The quality cost of 2-bit KV is not independent of the retained-token set. Mechanism: H2O-style eviction retains exactly the high-attention-mass tokens; KIVI-style per-token value quantization (chosen because attention is 84.3% sparse) confines error per token, so error on low-mass tokens is cheap — but pruning forces the 2-bit error onto the high-mass tokens the method most relies on. Conversely, the full-precision residual window (KIVI R=128) protects only recent tokens, and under aggressive pruning the important non-recent tokens are both *retained* (needed for quality) and *2-bit* (damaged) simultaneously. **Glue-semantics pre-registration (revision R1):** H2O eviction is always scored on FP16 attention (decoupled arm — the retained set is decided on FP16 attention, then quantization is applied to it, isolating "2-bit error lands on heavy hitters" from "2-bit changed the heavy hitters"); H2O's recent-window protection = 128 tokens = KIVI R (exact overlap); quantization groups are formed over retained tokens in position order with scales/zero-points recomputed per group after eviction; the residual queue holds the last 128 retained positions. Quantified anchor: the quality drop at 2-bit relative to 16-bit is ≥2× larger at 20% retention than at 60% retention, and the retention×bit-width interaction term in the quality regression is significant at α=0.05 with an effect ≥50% of the bit-width main effect, in at least one workload (per-workload verdicts reported separately; the ≥1-workload support vs both-workload kill asymmetry is a pre-registered design choice, revision R3).

**H2 — (retention, bit-width) × tier interaction (memory → latency translation).** The memory savings produced by axes 1 and 2 do not translate proportionally into tier-related latency. Mechanism: host-tier cost is a function of fetch volume (retained tokens × bytes/token), fetch count (chunk granularity), fixed per-fetch latency, and repair-driven refetches; halving tokens and halving bytes per token does not halve latency because (a) small transfers under-utilize the PCIe path, (b) 2-bit storage leaves full-precision residual buffers (KIVI R=128, GEAR n_b=20) whose size scales with retention, and (c) low-bit state may require dequantization or reconstruction (ShadowKV-style `K_sparse = A·B` rebuild) on the fetch path. Quantified anchor: the host-tier TTFT/TPOT penalty at the joint minimum (20% retention, 2-bit) exceeds 1.5× the sum of the two single-axis penalties measured from the full/16-bit/GPU-only baseline.

**H3 — Pareto reversal.** The configuration composed of each axis's individually-best setting (20% retention, 2-bit, GPU-only) is not on the Pareto frontier of the joint metric (quality × TTFT/TPOT × HBM usage). Mechanism: single-axis optima are chosen against single-axis baselines; their conjunction can either exceed the quality tolerance (bit error concentrated on retained tokens, per H1) or waste HBM that the tier could absorb (2-bit is unnecessary when host memory holds the cache), so some other cell of the matrix dominates it. Quantified anchor: among the 3×2×2×2 = 24 cells, the axis-best composition is dominated by ≥1 other cell with ≥10% improvement in at least one primary metric and ≤5% regression in every other, outside measurement error, replicated across seeds.

H1–H3 are ordered so that any single one can fail independently: H1 is a quality-regression test, H2 a latency-translation test, H3 a global ordering test. Three are needed because non-additivity could in principle appear in quality only (H1), in tier latency only (H2), or only in the joint ordering (H3).

# Independent Variables

1. **Retained-token budget** R ∈ {100%, 60%, 20%} — three levels. Anchors: H2O's default 20% (5× reduction) and its 60% collapse point for local/window baselines; GEAR's H2O-50% comparison; PyramidKV's 12% / 0.7% operating points. 20% is the aggressive corner; 60% is intermediate; 100% is the full-cache baseline.
2. **KV bit-width** B ∈ {16-bit (FP16), 2-bit} — two levels. 2-bit is implemented as the existing KIVI scheme (key per-channel, value per-token, group G=32, residual R=128) with hyperparameters fixed across all cells; 16-bit is the full-cache baseline. Two levels are sufficient because H1 requires only a high/low comparison and the hypotheses need no bit-width curvature; 4-bit is deliberately excluded to keep the matrix minimal (24 cells).
3. **Storage tier** T ∈ {GPU-only, GPU+host} — two levels. Host tier = either real host DRAM offload with a fixed chunk layout, or a calibrated tier emulator (nominal PCIe 3.0 x16: 31.5 GB/s transfer with measured per-fetch latency constants, calibrated against a real host run at one anchor cell as required by Confounders). FlexGen/InfiniGen/ShadowKV all use this exact boundary (A11; FlexGen additionally spans disk, which is outside this hypothesis).
4. **Workload** W ∈ {RAG multi-document QA (dispersed attention, e.g., LongBench Qasper/2WikiMQA/NarrativeQA), multi-step reasoning (dense dependence, e.g., GSM8K 8-shot CoT)} — two levels, chosen from T1's breakdown conditions ("retrieval-dense, reasoning... at aggressive budgets/bit-widths") and KIVI/GEAR's observed sensitivity split (LongBench near-lossless vs GSM8K needing the residual window / repair).

Full design: 3 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 24 cells, N ≥ 128 requests per cell (power pre-registered from a pilot, see Ambiguous Outcome). No factor beyond the four is added because no hypothesis requires it.

# Dependent Variables

- **TTFT** (median + P95): captures prefill, tier fetch, and reconstruction latency — required by H2 (host-tier penalty) and H3.
- **TPOT** (median + P95): captures per-step fetch, dequant/rebuild, and kernel effects in decode — required by H2; reported separately from TTFT because the phase dichotomy (A2) forbids aggregation.
- **Throughput** (tokens/s) and SLO attainment at the fixed quality tolerance: composite coordinate for H3's Pareto analysis.
- **HBM peak usage** (measured, not derived): realizes the memory savings of axes 1 and 2 — required by H3's memory coordinate and by the batching confounder (see below).
- **Transfer/fetch volume** (bytes + fetch count): isolates the tier mechanism from emulator artifacts — required by H2 to distinguish "bytes saved" from "latency saved".
- **Quality** (per-workload metric: F1/ROUGE for RAG; GSM8K accuracy for reasoning): required by H1 and by the quality-at-SLO goodput definition in H3.

# Control Variables

- **Model:** one Llama-2-7B (MHA, 4K context). Rationale: MHA avoids the GQA/MQA confound that already changes KIVI's behavior (Falcon MQA needs 4-bit); Llama-2-7B is evaluated by H2O, KIVI, GEAR, PyramidKV, and InfiniGen, so per-cell expectations are anchored in the corpus.
- **GPU:** one A100 80GB (or equivalent), clocks locked, warmup fixed. All papers' tier claims rest on GPU HBM ≈ 2 TB/s / PCIe ≈ 31.5 GB/s (ShadowKV's own constants), so this hardware makes the tier hypothesis testable at all.
- **Precision:** model weights FP16, no weight quantization — isolates KV effects (GEAR uses 8-bit weights only in its efficiency section; excluded here).
- **Serving framework:** one framework with paged attention (vLLM-class), same scheduler, fixed max batch 32 for all latency cells (safe for every cell), with a separate secondary max-batch sweep (the memory savings surface there, not as uncontrolled batch changes).
- **Request count, seed, output-length cap:** fixed per workload (N ≥ 128, seeds fixed, output capped, e.g., 256 tokens).
- **Quality tolerance:** pre-registered at ≤3% relative drop on the workload metric vs the (100%, 16-bit, GPU-only) baseline cell.
- **Chunk layout:** block size 16 tokens for paging; tier chunk size fixed (block-aligned, e.g., 512-token chunks) across all cells.

# Confounders

1. **Kernel efficiency differences between bit-widths:** 2-bit fused dequant-matmul kernels may have different FLOP/W-efficiency than FP16; a tier-latency interaction could be a kernel artifact. *Detect/neutralize:* microbenchmark each kernel at equal byte volume; additionally run quality metrics on a fake-quantization (dequantize-in-compute) variant to separate numerical error from kernel speed; report both.
2. **Residual / compensation buffers:** "2-bit" KIVI keeps R=128 tokens in FP16 and GEAR keeps an n_b=20 FP16 streaming buffer; nominal 2-bit is really mixed precision, and buffer size scales with retention. *Detect/neutralize:* account actual bytes (measured HBM + transferred bytes), never nominal bits; include buffer bytes in transfer-volume accounting so H2's mechanism test is honest.
3. **Batching changes:** memory savings can alter achievable batch size and thus throughput without any interaction. *Neutralize:* fixed concurrency (batch 32) for the main matrix; max-batch capacity reported as a separate measurement, not merged into the interaction analysis.
4. **Memory fragmentation / paging:** paged attention means retained-token savings do not map linearly to HBM usage. *Detect:* measure HBM directly (`cudaMemGetInfo`/driver API) rather than inferring from formulas; if fragmentation differs across cells, report and condition.
5. **Tier emulator fidelity:** an emulated PCIe path may miss real root-complex contention and fragmentation (T5). *Detect/neutralize:* calibrate the emulator against a real host-DRAM run at one anchor cell; if the TTFT mismatch exceeds 15%, tier results are treated as ordinal only and H2/H3 conclusions restricted accordingly (pre-registered).
6. **Quality metric saturation:** retrieval metrics saturate (e.g., PyramidKV TREC 66→66 across methods), hiding interactions. *Detect/neutralize:* include unsaturated per-task metrics (Qasper F1, GSM8K accuracy); pre-register that saturated cells (≥2% of variance across cells) are excluded from the quality analysis.
7. **Prefill/decode phase differences:** prefill is compute-bound, decode is bandwidth-bound (A2), so tier effects may appear in one phase only. *Neutralize:* TTFT and TPOT reported and analyzed separately; no aggregate latency variable.
8. **GPU warmup / frequency:** fixed warmup and locked clocks.
9. **Quantization calibration artifacts:** per-channel vs per-token choice and group size are fixed constants (KIVI G=32, R=128) across all cells — calibration is a controlled constant, not a factor, so it cannot masquerade as an interaction.

# Expected Observation If True

- **H1 true:** the quality drop at 2-bit relative to 16-bit is ~2× larger at 20% retention than at 60% (e.g., GSM8K accuracy loss ≈1–2 points at 60% vs ≥4 points at 20%); the retention×bit-width term in the quality regression is significant and its effect is ≥50% of the bit-width main effect; token-level analysis shows the largest per-token quantization errors landing on retained heavy-hitter tokens under aggressive pruning.
- **H2 true:** the host-tier TTFT penalty grows super-linearly as retention is lowered while bit-width is lowered — e.g., penalty(20%, 2-bit) > penalty(20%, 16-bit) + penalty(100%, 2-bit) by >1.5× — while transfer *volume* drops monotonically; fetch count and small-transfer latency, not volume, explain the gap (byte-normalized latency per cell is non-constant, violating A8).
- **H3 true:** the axis-best cell (20%, 2-bit, GPU-only) is dominated — e.g., (20%, 2-bit, GPU+host) improves HBM by >30% with ≤5% TTFT loss, or (60%, 2-bit, GPU-only) improves quality by ≥10% with ≤5% latency loss; the Pareto frontier contains no cell that is the per-axis best in more than one axis.

# Falsification Condition

- **Primary hypothesis:** falsified if, for every dependent variable and both workloads, all two-way and three-way interaction terms are non-significant (α=0.05, with Benjamini–Hochberg correction across metric×workload families) and R²_int_rel ≤ 0.10 (same denominator as support — revision R2). This is the additive-composition null; it is a legitimate, publishable outcome that reclassifies RQ-8 as engineering composition. **Gray zone (pre-registered):** a significant interaction with R²_int_rel ∈ [0.10, 0.50] is INCONCLUSIVE — neither supported nor falsified — and requires the E4 full-matrix run before any verdict. Note: an E3-level null is only "additive at R ∈ {20%, 100%} endpoints" (revision per critic D); the full primary falsification requires E4's 3×2×2×2 design.
- **H1:** falsified if the retention×bit-width interaction term on quality is non-significant at α=0.05 AND its effect size is <10% of the bit-width main effect in **both** workloads. (Support in only one workload is support for that workload only — reported per workload, revision R3.)
- **H2:** falsified if the host-tier TTFT/TPOT penalty at (20%, 2-bit) is ≤1.15× the sum of the single-axis penalties (i.e., additive within 15% measurement error, tolerance pilot-derived), or if byte-normalized transfer latency is constant across all cells (A8 holds — cost linear in bytes).
- **H3:** falsified if the axis-best combined cell IS on the Pareto frontier within measurement error — i.e., no other cell achieves ≥10% improvement in any primary metric with ≤5% regression in all others, replicated across ≥3 seeds and both workloads.
- **Overall null:** all three falsified ⇒ the axes compose additively under this workload × hardware × metric envelope; no compound mechanism is warranted by this corpus (stage2_final_review §6 G1: downgraded to engineering composition). The E1+E2a cheap-null path (plan) declares primary + H1 + H2 null only; H3 is not assessed at that stage because a Pareto reversal can arise from main effects alone.

# Ambiguous Outcome

- **Single-configuration regression explained by one axis:** if (20%, 2-bit) loses quality but (100%, 2-bit) loses similar quality, the drop is a bit-width main effect, not an interaction; H1 must not be scored from corner cells alone.
- **GPU-bound regime:** if prefill compute dominates every cell (short contexts, small batches), memory and tier effects are masked and H2/H3 are untestable regardless of truth; pre-registered remedy: the RAG workload uses 4K contexts and decode-heavy cells, and if the regime check fails, tier hypotheses are declared "not testable in this regime."
- **Quality tolerance too loose:** if every cell passes the ≤3% tolerance, H1 and H3's quality coordinates are uninformative; the tolerance is pre-registered so that at least one 2-bit cell is expected to fail on at least one workload (KIVI/GEAR numbers say GSM8K 2-bit without repair is the likely candidate).
- **Emulator fidelity failure:** if the tier emulator mismatches the real host anchor by >15%, tier results are ordinal-only and H2/H3 cannot discriminate; this is recorded, not papered over.
- **Point-corroboration trap:** H2O's "no compounding error" (H2O+Quant, OPT-30B, COPA/OpenBookQA/PiQA at 20%/4-bit/GPU-only/lm-eval) is a single-corner observation against a 4-bit, GPU-only, single-workload backdrop; it cannot discriminate any hypothesis in this matrix and must not be cited as evidence of additivity.
- **Median-only reporting:** interactions may live in P95 tails (T5: "fine-grained reads, small transfer units, high concurrency"); both medians and percentiles are reported, and a median-only regression with a tail interaction is recorded as ambiguous rather than as support.
- **Statistical power:** 24 cells with noisy TTFT can produce individually noisy interaction terms; N per cell is fixed from a pilot variance estimate, and any cell with effective N < 32 after failures is flagged and excluded, not interpolated.
