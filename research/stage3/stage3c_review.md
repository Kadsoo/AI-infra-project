## Review Standard and Evidence Boundary

A RQ could enter `KEEP` only if all of the following held:

1. Its locked hypothesis, baseline, metric, success condition, and falsification condition were documented before the relevant result.
2. The positive observation came from a fair matched comparison, with the target mechanism isolated from major workload/resource/scheduler changes.
3. The observation was stable across the pre-registered repetitions and controls, had a practically material effect, and was not merely a single synthetic corner.
4. The strongest alternative explanation was either directly ruled out or small enough to leave the conclusion intact.
5. The RQ was more than a restatement of a known limitation under a new name.

All four directories contain `LOCKED_PLAN.md` files with substantially clearer contracts than ordinary exploratory work. That is a strength. However, the workspace is not a Git repository and the locks have no immutable external timestamp; the freeze is therefore **documented local sequencing**, not independently auditable preregistration. This is a confidence limitation, not an accusation of post-hoc editing. See `research/stage3/stage3b_summary.md:46-50` and the per-RQ logs.

The review did not rely only on `stage3b_summary.md`. It checked the locked plans, result/report files, source code where the implementation was decisive, and the relevant aggregate/raw CSVs. Evidence labels below assess support for the stated positive hypothesis; a useful negative screen is called out separately rather than being inflated into positive evidence.

## RQ-01 — P/D vs Colocated Crossover

### What was claimed and what was actually tested

The frozen primary claim requires a cross-node 25 Gbps, bursty, equal-GPU P/D deployment to have P99 TTFT at least 1.5x the colocated baseline, with transfer plus queueing material on the critical path (`RQ_01/LOCKED_PLAN.md:9-19`).

Stage 3B ran:

- E1: a 108-cell analytic cost model, whose support anchor is a modeled 41.8% P95 transfer fraction and a 71.6% 25-Gbps-vs-NVLink gap (`processed/e1_report.md:20-32`; `raw/e1_anchor.json`).
- E2: an NVLink-class queue simulator intended to isolate H2 (`processed/e2_report.md:5-12`).
- No E3/E4/E5 transfer microbenchmark, matched serving harness, GPU-allocation sweep, goodput comparison, or real cross-node network experiment (`experiment_log.md:27-29`).

### Experiment integrity

| Check | Review finding |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis precommitment | **Partial.** Main thresholds are frozen, but the planned ON/OFF burst process was replaced by Gamma-renewal arrivals, and the probe expanded to six points without proving an SLO boundary. More importantly, H2 is reported as rejected even though its own frozen falsification conjunction requires both `P99 ratio <= 1.1` and queue fraction `<15%`; the report records queue fractions around 0.93–1.00 instead. See `LOCKED_PLAN.md:37-45`, `experiment_log.md:20-25`, and `processed/e2_report.md:34-59`. |
| Baseline fairness | **Fail.** `e2_sim.py` gives P/D two prefill/transfer-engine pairs, whereas colocated has two single-server queues; neither side models decode execution or consumes an output length (`code/e2_sim.py:21-40`, `140-208`). Thus the claimed equal-GPU, same-serving-workload comparison cannot be inferred from the simulator. The colocated side also hard-codes a 25% chunking penalty at `tau=1024` without a matching calibration (`code/e2_sim.py:9-13`, `168-170`). |
| Variable isolation | **Fail.** E1 assumes transfer is always critical-path and has zero overlap; E2 tests NVLink rather than the 25-Gbps joint condition. The E1 result can therefore be produced by the model's serialization rule, rather than a general P/D failure. |
| Repetition | Five simulator seeds exist, but they repeat one synthetic trace and cannot repair the resource/cache/decode omissions. |
| Cherry picking | The positive E1 anchor uses effective-bandwidth factor `f=0.24`; only 12 of 27 long/P95 model cells pass both locked support lines, while the sweep ranges from 4.63% to 72.01% transfer fraction. The actual crossover around intermediate topology is not measured. |
| Raw traceability | **Fail.** The recorded `e2_sim.py` hash is `9e8f...`, while the current source hashes to `26a110...`; the raw E2 CSVs predate the current source. In addition, `e2_run.py:95-114` groups summaries without `transfer_scale` but labels them with the first scale, so `raw/e2_summary.csv` mixes three transfer scales while presenting a single one. |

### Hypothesis-level evidence

| Hypothesis | Evidence strength | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|
| H1 — transfer materiality | **Weak** | A conditional analytic signal only. No measured transfer path or serving TTFT verifies it. |
| H2 — cache-affine queue hotspot | **None** | The simulator's directional result cannot adjudicate H2 because the frozen kill condition did not fire and the P/D/colocated comparison is not a valid serving comparison. |
| H3 — baseline overtake/goodput | **None** | Not run. No goodput, GPU parity, TPOT, or allocation result exists. |

### Effect size, generality, alternative explanation, and literature

- **Effect size:** modeled as large only at one selected E1 anchor; no observed system-level crossover exists. The practical effect is therefore **not established**.
- **Generality:** **hardware-/implementation-specific and synthetic-only.** One trace, assumed critical-path transfer, and a simplified service model do not establish a general operating boundary.
- **Strongest alternative explanation:** the 41.8% E1 fraction follows from forced serial transfer/zero overlap, while E2 makes P/D and colocated execute different abstract workloads. Neither alternative is excluded. A real matched-resource 1P+1D versus colocated harness, including output/decode work, calibrated chunking, equal warm cache state, fixed burst trace, and a rate/topology sweep would be a new Stage 3A experiment—not a small Stage 4 confirmation.
- **Prior work:** **Known but underexplored.** DistServe already makes placement bandwidth-aware and explicitly uses co-location to avoid transfer cost (`paper_notes/DistServe.md:31-42`); FlowKV already reports a long-context transfer contribution to end-to-end latency (`paper_notes/FlowKV.md:14-16`). A rigorous crossover measurement could be useful, but this record has not supplied one.

**Final classification: DROP — Unsupported.**

## RQ-02 — Static Budget Fragility

### What was claimed and what was actually tested

The primary claim requires a fixed published setting to fail on out-of-suite conditions while a same-method, same-implementation, same-measured-budget oracle does materially better (`RQ_02/LOCKED_PLAN.md:9-19`). It requires paired quality, TPOT/kernel, HBM, and oracle evidence (`LOCKED_PLAN.md:21-35`).

Only E1 ran: a desk re-extraction of paper-note tables. The RQ itself states that E2–E5 and all matched-harness measurements were blocked (`RQ_02/result.md:11-19`, `59-65`). The E1 table is useful for pruning, but it is not an experiment of H1, H2, or H3.

### Experiment integrity

| Check | Review finding |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis precommitment | **Partial pass.** The contract is specific and no post-E1 threshold drift is apparent, but local timestamps are not immutable preregistration. |
| Baseline fairness | **Not executed.** Within-paper pairs are reasonable for a screening ledger, but no common harness has matched model, task, template, batch, HBM, or kernel path. |
| Variable isolation | **Fail for the planned evidence.** The proposed phase condition changes task subset, output cap, and generation phase together; it cannot isolate retained-state phase failure from generic long-generation error. |
| Repetition | **None.** There are no model runs, per-example outputs, timing repetitions, or raw benchmark results. |
| Cherry picking | All legally relevant out-of-suite H1 cells are marked `measure` rather than observed. Therefore published, favorable in-suite rows cannot establish the cross-condition claim. |
| Data integrity | Arithmetic/transcription triage is largely auditable, but the ledger has 52 rows and 11 `measure_flagged` rows while surrounding documents state 54 and 10. More importantly, the most prominent H3 anchor is categorized incorrectly. |

### Critical invalid H3 anchor

Frozen H3 requires retuning a method's **own** knobs in the same implementation at the same measured budget (`LOCKED_PLAN.md:19, 31-35`). Yet `processed/E1_triage_matrix.csv:37` treats `27.75 (prefill-only SnapKV/PyramidKV)` to `52.17 (prefill+decode SCOPE Slide)` as an 88% SCOPE oracle gain. The value is correctly transcribed from `research/paper_notes/SCOPE.md:104`, but it is a method-family comparison, not same-method retuning. The same-family selection-frequency row is only prior-work evidence and has no measured-HBM record.

### Hypothesis-level evidence

| Hypothesis | Evidence strength | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|
| H1 — out-of-suite quality fragility | **None** | Every decisive out-of-suite condition is unmeasured. |
| H2 — TPOT/kernel overhead | **None** | SCOPE's published +100% TPOT anchor is not reproduced in the locked harness. |
| H3 — equal-budget oracle gap | **None** | The 88% headline is cross-method; no equal-budget oracle run exists. |

### Effect size, generality, alternative explanation, and literature

- **Effect size:** individual prior-work effects are sometimes large—e.g., SCOPE's 36.57 versus 18.28 tok/s table row—but Stage 3 has no new prevalence or common-harness effect estimate. **Not established.**
- **Generality:** **implementation-/hardware-/workload-specific.** The RQ combines heterogeneous retention, quantization, and phase mechanisms without executing a comparable matrix.
- **Strongest alternative explanation:** any observed difference would be a known method-specific compression or kernel trade-off, or generic autoregressive error accumulation, rather than a fixed setting failing because of task/phase change. The current work does not distinguish these mechanisms.
- **Prior work:** **Already known** at mechanism level. SCOPE documents phase behavior, KIVI documents MQA sensitivity, and PyramidKV documents budget sensitivity. A clean matched cross-method boundary is only **known but underexplored**, not demonstrated here.

**Final classification: DROP — Unsupported.** It needs multiple design repairs plus the unexecuted central matrix; GPU access alone is not a decisive control for the current composite RQ.

## RQ-04 — Cache Affinity vs Per-Class Tail/Fairness

### What was claimed and what was actually tested

The frozen primary claim requires cache affinity to provide a memory-accounted hit-rate gain and at least 20% aggregate-mean TTFT improvement, while creating a hot-class P99 hotspot with the cold class protected (`RQ_04/LOCKED_PLAN.md:9-19`, `31-45`).

E0–E3 are large CPU simulations/replays. They include 10 seeded traces per reported cell and retained aggregate CSVs. That establishes that the stated FIFO simulator can reproduce its own behavior. It does **not** establish a real serving bottleneck.

### Experiment integrity

| Check | Review finding |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis precommitment | **Partial pass.** H1–H3, controls, and thresholds were explicitly locked. However, no immutable provenance exists, and post-result language promotes a residual H2 observation after the primary H1 conjunction failed. |
| Baseline fairness | **Fail for the claimed trade-off.** E0 finds a 0.00 pp hit-rate gain, and E1 finds affinity's aggregate mean TTFT is 1.68–2.69x load-only rather than <=0.8x (`result.md:26-39`). The experiment therefore does not exhibit a reuse benefit to trade against a tail cost. |
| Variable isolation | **Fail.** The A policy is force-warmed: the first 200 hot completions are sent to node 0. The log explicitly says that without this, the prefix spreads to all nodes and A degenerates to B (`logs/experiment_log.md:91-97`). The model is one FIFO prefill server per node with linear prefill cost, permanent hot-prefix retention, and no continuous batching or GPU kernel behavior. |
| Repetition | Ten seeds per cell are useful **simulation** repetitions only. E3's bit-identical reruns show code determinism, not independent scientific replication. Request-level provenance is incomplete: `stage3b_summary.md:48` claims about 7,500 request logs, while `raw/per_request/` contains 1,901 files. |
| Cherry picking | The headline base grid uses Poisson, gamma=1, fixed load 0.8, L1, and a single shared hot prefix. The control variants show these conditions are decisive, not incidental. |
| Result/report consistency | **Fail.** `result.md` calls H2 gamma/burst robust, but raw controls contradict it at the headline cell. |

### Direct raw-data contradiction for the reported H2 robustness

At `policy=A, p_hot=.8, c=32, load=.8, L1`:

- In `raw/e2_cells.csv` at `gamma=1`, the ten base repetitions yield hot P99 around 6.01 s and cold attainment 1.0000 on average. This is a narrow FIFO-model signal.
- In `raw/e2_gamma_robust.csv` at `gamma=2`, mean hot P99 rises to **12.0431 s** but mean cold attainment collapses to **0.5024**, so none of the ten runs satisfies H2's cold >=99% condition.
- In `raw/e2_burst.csv`, the same cell has cold attainment **0.9885** at CV2 and **0.5933** at CV4. It therefore fails the claimed protected-cold-class condition under both burst controls.

The alleged `c*=16` boundary is also not a stable hot-tail crossover. In `raw/e2_cells.csv` at `A, p_hot=.7, load=.8, L1, gamma=1`, mean hot P99 is 6.67/6.58/6.59/6.59/6.59 s for c=8/16/32/64/128. What changes sharply is admission/gate waiting and cold-class protection, not a monotone hot-tail transition due to a demonstrated affinity-versus-reuse mechanism.

### Hypothesis-level evidence

| Hypothesis | Evidence strength | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|
| H1 — reuse gain plus hot-node queue hotspot | **None** | The hit-rate and aggregate-mean legs fail, so the stated reuse-versus-tail trade-off is not observed. |
| H2 — per-class SLO boundary | **Weak** | A narrow synthetic FIFO signal exists at gamma=1/Poisson, but the strongest alternative explanation is not excluded and robustness is contradicted by raw controls. |
| H3 — attribution and controllability | **None** | Threshold replication restores the target only in 1 of 13 active cells; the frozen H3 fails. |

### Effect size, generality, alternative explanation, and literature

- **Effect size:** **large in the simulator** (e.g., several-second hot P99 and multi-x ratios), but unestablished on an actual serving engine. The absence of a hit-rate gain makes the modeled harm less actionable than the report presents.
- **Generality:** **synthetic-only and implementation-specific.** One forced cache-placement model and FIFO queue cannot establish a general cache-affinity fairness bottleneck.
- **Strongest alternative explanation:** the forced A-only warm-up, permanent one-node hot prefix, and per-node FIFO queue directly cause concentration even when affinity brings no extra reuse. The experiment does not rule this out; it documents it.
- **Prior work:** the broad concern is **already known**. SGLang notes that greedy longest-prefix/cache-aware scheduling can cause starvation and leaves fair scheduling as future work (`paper_notes/SGLang.md:200-202`). A per-class real-system measurement gap may be **known but underexplored**, but it is not a new bottleneck established by this simulation.

**Final classification: DROP — Experimental Artifact.** Reviving it requires a new `LOCKED_PLAN_v2` and a new Stage 3A control: a real multi-GPU engine with randomized equal cache placement, no A-only priming, measured batching/service cost, and a pre-registered requirement that hot harm and cold >=99% both persist under Poisson and burst traces. Current E4 cannot be treated as confirmation because the frozen pipeline already rejects H1 and H3.

## RQ-08 — Token x Bit-width x Tier Non-Additivity

### What was claimed and what was actually tested

The frozen primary hypothesis is a material factorial interaction in a matched single-7B serving harness; it requires TTFT/TPOT/throughput/HBM/quality measurements and Type-III interaction analysis (`RQ_08/LOCKED_PLAN.md:9-23`).

Only E1 executed: a deterministic, CPU-only cost model using published constants and the FlexGen `T=max(I/O, compute)` rule. It produced 48 model rows—3 retention levels x 2 bit-widths x 2 tiers x 2 workloads x 2 per-fetch constants—and no serving, kernel, quality, HBM, or factorial experiment (`RQ_08/result.md:12-16`, `processed/e1_report.md:1-12`). The script hash matches the log, so this is a reproducible calculation; it is not a measurement of the claimed mechanisms.

### Experiment integrity

| Check | Review finding |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis precommitment | **Partial pass.** The revised lock clearly specifies baseline, statistics, success/falsification, and glue semantics. The lack of immutable time anchoring remains a common limitation. |
| Baseline fairness | **Not applicable as a serving comparison.** Cells share one algebraic model, but no actual identical-kernel/hardware/workload baseline exists. |
| Variable isolation | **Fail for the primary RQ.** The E1 result is driven by assumed PCIe/HBM bandwidth, temporal locality, 512-token chunking, a fixed KIVI residual, and the max-overlap rule. It cannot identify real dequantization, fragmentation, root-complex contention, scheduler, or concurrency effects. |
| Repetition | Deterministic recomputation only; no independent runs. |
| Cherry picking | The two workload points are 4K RAG and 900-token GSM8K. The latter is tier-untestable under the model, leaving one usable workload regime. |

### Hypothesis-level evidence

| Hypothesis | Evidence strength | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|
| H1 — retention x bit-width quality | **None** | No quality experiment was run. |
| H2 — joint corner tier super-additivity | **None for support; weak model-bound negative screen** | The model gives corner penalty 0 and raw-transfer S=0.155–0.312, but this is not a real-machine result. |
| H3 — Pareto reversal | **None** | No quality/latency/HBM coordinates exist. |
| Primary factorial interaction | **None** | The full matrix and ANOVA were not run. |

### Effect size, generality, alternative explanation, and literature

- **Effect size:** for the claimed joint-minimum bottleneck, **negligible in this cost model**. The reported 28.8–29.3% byte-normalized variation occurs at high-retention 2-bit host cells, not the H2 corner (`processed/e1_report.md:84-117`). No end-to-end effect size is measured.
- **Generality:** **synthetic-only / implementation-specific.** It is tied to Llama-2-7B MHA, KIVI G=32/R=128, the selected chunk size, and published constants.
- **Strongest alternative explanation:** real per-fetch latency, overlap, contention, fragmentation, and concurrency can all produce or hide a tier interaction differently from the algebra. The report itself lists these as unmodeled (`RQ_08/result.md:52-57`); none is excluded.
- **Prior work:** **already explained at mechanism level.** The landscape already contains hybrid retention/compression/offload systems, and GEAR documents residual/group overhead rather than treating nominal bit-width as actual bytes. The E1 residual accounting is a useful implementation note, not evidence of a new research bottleneck.

**Final classification: DROP — Unsupported.** The H2 negative screen is useful for avoiding an expensive unmotivated tier study, but it does not validate any new RQ or justify Stage 4.
